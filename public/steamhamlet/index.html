STEAMHAMLET → TurtleBlock AI

STEAMHAMLET is the earlier room of possibilities behind TurtleBlock AI: a shared learning environment where ideas and informational objects can be moved, changed, scaled, remixed, juxtaposed, tested, and revised.

TurtleBlock AI takes that long-running educational vision and gives it a more immediately executable playground: conversational agents, WorldSpec, Minecraft, and a public build process.
