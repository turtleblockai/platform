TurtleBlock AI + RE/EDUCATION

TurtleBlock AI is being developed as part of the educational practice, research, and school-building work of RE/EDUCATION.

A school can also be a laboratory.
TurtleBlock AI provides a place to design, test, revise, and study new forms of learner-centered computational experience while those experiences are being built.

Credentialed practice meets research

The project connects day-to-day work as a credentialed educator with longer-running doctoral and post-doctoral inquiry into constructivism, constructionism, digital learning environments, Minecraft, artificial intelligence, and co-active emergence.

That means TurtleBlock AI is not being developed as a detached software demo. It is intended as a functional playground for actual educational work: learners making things, teachers participating in inquiry, and the platform itself changing in response.

Related work

RE/EDUCATION is one part of a larger public trail that includes STEAMHAMLET, Critical Techno Constructivism, Minecraft-centered research and writing, and the evolving WorldSpec language.
