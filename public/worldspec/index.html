WorldSpec

WorldSpec is the edition-independent representation layer between a learner’s idea, a conversation with Turtle, and a world that can be built, inhabited, tested, reflected upon, and revised.

learner intent → agent dialogue → WorldSpec → build → inhabit → notice → reflect → revise ↺
Schema

Objects, relationships, form, function, materials, meaning, constraints, reflection, and history.

Lexicon

A shared vocabulary for create, move, scale, remix, preserve, test, inspect, reflect, fork, restore, and more.

Interaction grammar

Rules for direct action, interpretation, clarification, ambiguity, and reflection.

Not an AI world vending machine

The learner remains the designer and producer. Concrete requests can become direct operations. Interpretive ideas—“welcoming,” “authoritarian,” “sacred,” “communal”—should remain visible as interpretations and may trigger questions instead of silent execution.

Public specification

The machine-readable schema, lexicon, grammar, and examples are developed in public in the TurtleBlock AI repository.
