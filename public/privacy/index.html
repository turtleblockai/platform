Privacy Notice

TurtleBlock AI is an experimental educational research and development project.

The Playground is designed for inquiry, prototyping, and research. Do not submit information you would not want processed as part of an experimental research system.

What the Playground receives

When you submit material through the TurtleBlock AI Playground, the system receives the text you submit, the version of WorldSpec and the privacy notice associated with the interaction, and a random browser session identifier that may connect related revisions. The public Playground does not require you to identify yourself.

How Playground submissions may be used

Playground submissions may be processed to operate, evaluate, research, develop, and improve TurtleBlock AI, Turtle, WorldSpec, related educational tools, and future research or evaluation datasets. Automated systems may process submissions, and material may also be reviewed by people involved in TurtleBlock AI research and development.

Research-data pipeline

TurtleBlock AI separates a raw Playground submission from an approved research or training dataset record. The planned research-data pipeline screens submissions, creates a redacted copy when appropriate, assigns review and dataset states, and requires an explicit review decision before material is treated as approved dataset material. Automated screening and redaction are imperfect and should not be relied upon to protect information that should not have been submitted in the first place.

Research persistence is activated only when the project database is configured. Until that infrastructure is enabled, a Playground interaction may be interpreted and returned without being written to the TurtleBlock AI research database.

Please do not submit personal or sensitive information

This includes names or contact information you wish to keep private, passwords or authentication tokens, financial information, medical information, confidential school records, private communications, government identifiers, or information about other people that you do not have permission to share.

Anonymous sessions and infrastructure metadata

A random browser session identifier may be stored so related interactions and revisions can be studied together. TurtleBlock AI does not intentionally place IP addresses into the Playground research dataset. Hosting, security, networking, and other infrastructure providers may independently process ordinary technical metadata necessary to provide and protect their services.

Discord and community conversation

Ordinary conversation in a TurtleBlock AI Discord server or community channel is not automatically treated as a TurtleBlock AI research-dataset submission. If a Discord command or interaction is later designed to send material into the TurtleBlock research pipeline, that research interaction should be clearly identified and handled under the applicable consent and privacy flow. Discord itself is a third-party service and may process account, message, device, network, and usage information under its own terms and privacy practices.

Third-party services

TurtleBlock AI may connect with services such as Discord, Cloudflare, GitHub, Minecraft or Microsoft services, and model or infrastructure providers as the project develops. Those services operate under their own terms and privacy practices. TurtleBlock AI will aim to keep the boundary between community conversation, application operation, and research-dataset use explicit.

Consent and changes

Before submitting through a research-enabled Playground flow, you may be asked to acknowledge the applicable privacy notice. The version you accepted may be recorded with the submission. Because TurtleBlock AI is actively being developed, this notice may change as features, integrations, and research practices evolve. Material changes should be reflected by a new notice version.

Project context

TurtleBlock AI is a build-in-public educational research project associated with RE/EDUCATION and the ongoing educational and doctoral research of Dr. Bryan P. Sanders / @nayrbgo.
