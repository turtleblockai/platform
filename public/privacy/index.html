Playground Privacy Notice

TurtleBlock AI is an experimental educational research and development project.

When you submit text or other material through the TurtleBlock AI Playground, your submission may be retained and analyzed to operate, evaluate, research, develop, and improve TurtleBlock AI, WorldSpec, related educational tools, and future research or training datasets.

Please do not submit personal or sensitive information.

This includes names or contact information you wish to keep private, passwords, financial information, medical information, confidential school records, private communications, or information about other people that you do not have permission to share.

How submissions may be used

Submissions may be processed by automated systems and may also be reviewed by people involved in TurtleBlock AI research and development. Before submitted material is incorporated into a research, evaluation, or training dataset, TurtleBlock AI intends to use automated and, where appropriate, human review to identify and remove or exclude personal, sensitive, unsafe, offensive, or irrelevant content. These processes are not perfect and cannot guarantee that every such item will be detected.

Anonymous sessions

The public Playground does not require you to identify yourself. The browser may assign a random anonymous session identifier so that related interactions and revisions can be studied together. TurtleBlock AI does not intentionally collect or store IP addresses as part of the Playground research dataset.

Consent

Before submitting through the Playground, you will be asked to acknowledge this notice. The version of the notice you accepted may be recorded with your submission.

Project context

TurtleBlock AI is a build-in-public educational research project associated with RE/EDUCATION and the ongoing educational and doctoral research of Dr. Bryan P. Sanders / @nayrbgo.
