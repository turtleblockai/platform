## Sanders ontology comparison

The immutable 2019 dissertation Dedoose source layer is unchanged. Today's work is a later operational TurtleBlock mapping only.

Relevant original Dedoose codes:

- **Freedom and Individuality** — collaborators retain distinct inquiry and attention states.
- **Shared Democracy** — common space does not imply common priority or compulsory agreement.
- **Problem Posing Education** — a shared event may become a question without the machine deciding what everyone should ask.
- **Predetermined Outcomes** — consensus must not become the hidden target state.
- **Social Impact** — visibility, disclosure, interruption, and participation rules distribute power.
- **Pedagogy** — when to surface, defer, disclose, negotiate, or remain plural changes the learning environment.
- **Engagement** — participation should not be reduced to synchronized attention.

Later Sanders concepts:

- **Co-active Emergence** — difference may alter the shared trajectory without being normalized away.
- **Possible Possibles** — several legitimate directions may remain live simultaneously.
- **Purposeful Play** — participants may wander differently in the same environment.
- **STEAMHAMLET** — a room of possibilities can hold multiple foregrounds.
- **Invisible Ideas Become Visible and Manipulable** — attention rules, consent, and disagreement become inspectable artifacts.
- **Machine Responses as Material for Evaluation** — Turtle's proposal or attempted group rule is itself material to inspect.
- **Cohabitation Without Provenance Collapse** — participant purpose, private rationale, shared rule, Turtle proposal, synthetic fixture, and system decision remain distinguishable.

## Seven established CTC domains

This contribution is system-research infrastructure, not evidence of learner learning.

1. **Personal Inquiry — primary.** Hostile cases preserve participant-specific purpose and foreground even inside one shared world.
2. **Compelling Problem or Question — supports.** The same event can be compelling to one person, deferred by another, and irrelevant to a third.
3. **Technology as Tool to Think With — primary.** Foreground, disclosure, consent, and shared-rule state become deterministic, inspectable computational material.
4. **Formative Demonstration of Learning — supports.** Changes in shared-rule participation might later help interpret inquiry development, but this test suite makes no learning claim.
5. **Reflection as Learning — supports.** Different participants may reflect at different times; no forced synchronization is encoded.
6. **Social and Cultural Critique — primary.** The suite tests who can make a concern collective, who can refuse, what stays private, and whether system logic can manufacture group importance.
7. **Sharing and Collaborating — primary.** The contribution directly tests collaboration with disagreement, selective disclosure, partial participation, withdrawal, and no-consensus states.

## Hostile suite

The suite contains eleven cases.

Valid cases preserve:

1. different legitimate foregrounds with no shared rule;
2. a temporary human-negotiated shared watchpoint with complete consent;
3. selective disclosure where rationale remains private;
4. a Turtle-proposed shared watchpoint that remains only a proposal.

Intentionally invalid cases reject:

1. machine averaging of participant priorities into consensus;
2. a Turtle-authored active group rule;
3. an active rule missing consent from an affected participant;
4. delivery to a non-consenting participant;
5. synthetic Turtle production execution;
6. the assumption that sharing a world implies shared attention;
7. use of the foreground wrapper to authorize new collection.

Core invariant:

> **A shared world does not imply a shared foreground; negotiated attention requires human consent and preserves plurality.**

## Unresolved / uncaptured residue

- Collaboration may be productive without consensus. This fits across Sharing and Collaborating, Social and Cultural Critique, Personal Inquiry, and purposeful play, but the framework does not explicitly formalize **durable disagreement** as a positive collaborative state.
- Selective disclosure still leaks one fact: that something matters to someone. Existence itself may be sensitive.
- Partial group rules raise a technical / pedagogical distinction between **coordination** and **universal participation**.
- A machine can preserve contradictory states deterministically, but representation alone does not show whether people experience that plurality as useful, burdensome, liberating, confusing, or socially costly.

No eighth CTC domain is proposed from this single cycle.
