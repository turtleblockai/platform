Overlap: Interaction Capture already treats rejection, correction, revision, and ambiguous reaction as different evidence classes, while Initiative Authority treats quieting/revocation as scoped authority changes. The Refusal Scope regression connects them: **negative evidence has provenance and an object; it is not a personality trait or global instruction unless the learner actually makes it one.**

Why it matters: persistent systems can make two opposite mistakes — forget a real refusal and keep pestering, or overremember a local refusal and suppress future possibility. Both are authority errors.

Uncertainty: exact structural scope does not prove that a learner experiences future quiet or resurfacing as respectful. Natural-language deixis such as “not that” may still leave the refused object unresolved.

Disposition: `relationship`, `test_candidate`, `next_edge_candidate`.
