## Fresh primary-source hunt

### OpenAI — recurring AI work is shaped by task division and human context

**Date:** September 16, 2026  
**Source:** OpenAI, *How workers are unlocking new ways of working*  
https://openai.com/index/unlocking-new-ways-of-working/

OpenAI reports that cross-occupation AI tasks can become recurring workflows and emphasizes how work is divided and carried out alongside tool access.

**Matters:** capability alone does not determine meaning or authority. Human context and task design govern how an AI contribution enters a larger workflow.

**Does not justify:** treating recurrence as permission for generalized personal memory, silent preference learning, or machine authority over a learner-owned trajectory.

### NVIDIA — evaluate the trace and the resulting environment state

**Date:** September 21, 2026  
**Source:** NVIDIA, *How to Evaluate AI Agents From Tool Calls to Task Completion*  
https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/how-to-evaluate-ai-agents-from-tool-calls-to-task-completion/

NVIDIA distinguishes step-level process traces from end-to-end environment-state verification and argues that plausible language is insufficient evidence that an agent actually completed a task.

**Matters:** a cross-branch proposal should be inspectable as a trace, and any accepted application should appear as an explicit new target revision rather than as a narrative claim that transfer occurred.

**Does not justify:** making task success, speed, or another metric the authority to decide whether one branch should influence another.

## Seven established CTC operational domains

1. **Personal Inquiry — primary.** A learner or human collaborator decides whether borrowed material belongs in the target inquiry.
2. **Compelling Problem or Question — primary.** The most useful transferable artifact may be a question rather than a solution.
3. **Technology as Tool to Think With — primary.** Branches and transfer proposals become manipulable computational objects rather than automatic synchronization.
4. **Formative Demonstration of Learning — supports.** Revision history may later contribute evidence, but accepting or rejecting a transfer is not itself a learning claim.
5. **Reflection as Learning — primary.** People may accept, rewrite, reject, or reinterpret a proposed perturbation.
6. **Social and Cultural Critique — primary.** Claims that one branch corrects another encode power, normativity, and assumptions about whose criteria travel.
7. **Sharing and Collaborating — primary.** Sharing can move a traceable difference without requiring merge or consensus.

No new CTC domain is proposed.

## Candidates considered

- **Automatic cross-branch synchronization — rejected.** It collapses proposal into execution and branch exchange into hidden merge.
- **Branch similarity/convergence score — rejected.** It would smuggle in a machine-defined amount of acceptable difference before the research warrants such a construct.
- **Live learner-facing cross-pollination UI — rejected today.** The semantics should survive hostile evaluation first.
- **Generalized persistent learner preference memory — rejected.** Unnecessary for the edge and outside current privacy/version scope.
- **Recursive Turtle applying transfers — rejected.** Synthetic self-play may generate fixtures or proposals but has no production authority.
- **Cross-Branch Perturbation Envelope + hostile suite — selected.** It is the smallest reversible artifact that directly answers the inherited horizon.

The contract preserves:

```text
source branch + source revision + source actor/evidence
  → traceable question / observation / constraint
  → Turtle or synthetic proposal with no execution authority

  → optional new target revision
```

## Hostile regression suite / observable success criterion

Fifteen deterministic cases are included: six valid and nine intentionally invalid.

Passing cases include a pending proposal, explicit human acceptance, human rewriting, human rejection, a world-observation proposal that still lacks authority, and a synthetic proposal that remains synthetic.

Failing cases reject source/target collapse, lost source provenance, fabricated synthetic approval, application while human authorization is pending, merge, correction claims, world authority, overwriting the current target revision, and loss of upstream human lineage.

The workflow is wired to run `npm run validate:cross-branch-perturbation` alongside the existing contract suites.

Core invariant:

> **A branch may donate a traceable question, observation, or constraint, but only human authority may decide whether that proposal changes a human-owned target branch; transfer is neither merge nor correction.**
