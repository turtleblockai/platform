## Fresh primary-source hunt

### OpenAI — work design and human context matter alongside capability

**Date:** September 16, 2026  
**Source:** OpenAI, *How workers are unlocking new ways of working*  
https://openai.com/index/unlocking-new-ways-of-working/

OpenAI's economic research reports that workers can incorporate cross-occupation AI tasks into recurring workflows and emphasizes that **how work is divided and carried out** deserves attention alongside AI access. People often bring relevant context or examples and ask AI to help apply or verify knowledge outside their usual role.

**Matters for TurtleBlock:** a useful computational workflow is not simply a model ranking outputs. Human framing, task division, context, and repeated revision shape what the AI contribution means.

**Does not justify:** treating workplace recurrence as learner evidence; importing occupational analytics into learner profiling; persistent personal memory.

### NVIDIA — shared coordination does not require uniform local authority

**Date:** September 15, 2026  
**Source:** NVIDIA, *Scaling Federated Learning Across Docker, Kubernetes, and Slurm with NVIDIA FLARE*  
https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/scaling-federated-learning-across-docker-kubernetes-and-slurm-with-nvidia-flare/

NVIDIA FLARE separates federation coordination from local execution so different sites can participate in one shared system while retaining control over their datasets, approved images, secrets, schedules, and resource policies.

**Matters:** heterogeneous participants can coordinate without being normalized into one local configuration. That is an engineering analogue for branch comparison: shared comparison does not require one canonical branch state or one authority over all branches.

**Does not justify:** treating federated infrastructure as pedagogical evidence; making privacy/local authority merely a technical deployment choice; automatic cross-branch synchronization.

### Minecraft Education — immersive worlds can make competing perspectives consequential

**Date:** September 14, 2026  
**Source:** Minecraft Education, *Every Voice Matters in Village Square*  
https://education.minecraft.net/en-us/discover/village-square-civics

Village Square asks learners to encounter different perspectives, balance fairness and representation, make community decisions, shape a village charter, and reflect on consequences through play. The experience deliberately includes compromise as an authored civic-learning goal.

**Matters:** divergent perspectives can become materially consequential inside an inhabitable world and later support reflection.

**Does not justify:** making compromise, convergence, or consensus TurtleBlock's default collaborative objective. Village Square's convergence goal is part of its authored curricular purpose.

### MIT Media Lab — measurable behavior can diverge from self-stated values

**Date:** September 18, 2026  
**Source:** MIT Media Lab, *Paying Attention to Values in the Age of Algorithmic Amplifiers*  
https://www.media.mit.edu/events/bonus-seminar-paying-attention-to-values-in-the-age-of-algorithmic-amplifiers/

The seminar describes misalignment between algorithmic amplification and users' self-stated values and discusses collective goals such as diversity and autotelicity in human creativity.

**Matters:** easy-to-measure behavior or ranking signals are not neutral stand-ins for human purpose or value. A branch can perform better on a metric without thereby becoming the better world.

**Does not justify:** inferring learner values from behavior; assigning hidden value profiles; treating value alignment as a single scalar objective.

The immutable dissertation Dedoose layer remains unchanged. Today's work is a later operational mapping.

Relevant original codes include **Freedom and Individuality**, **Problem Posing Education**, **Predetermined Outcomes**, **Shared Democracy**, **Social Impact**, **Pedagogy**, and **Observations on Life Itself**. Later Sanders/TurtleBlock concepts engaged:

- **Machine Responses as Material for Evaluation** — Turtle comparison remains inspectable material rather than verdict;
- **Purposeful Play** — a strange or inefficient branch can remain generative;
- **STEAMHAMLET** — a room of possibilities need not rank every possible future;
- **Cohabitation Without Provenance Collapse** — branch history, world evidence, human criterion, reflection, and Turtle interpretation remain distinct.

## Seven established CTC operational domains

1. **Personal Inquiry — primary.** Human purpose determines which consequences and criteria matter.
2. **Compelling Problem or Question — primary.** Divergent branch consequences may generate new questions without resolving into a winner.
3. **Technology as Tool to Think With — primary.** Branches and evidence become manipulable objects for comparison rather than answer-producing endpoints.
4. **Formative Demonstration of Learning — supports.** Branch revisions and reflections may later support formative interpretation, but branch performance alone is not learning evidence.
5. **Reflection as Learning — primary.** People can compare, reject comparison, revise criteria, or preserve plurality.
6. **Social and Cultural Critique — primary.** Ranking criteria can smuggle in efficiency, cultural, aesthetic, or power assumptions.
7. **Sharing and Collaborating — primary.** Collaboration can include comparison without compulsory convergence.

No new CTC domain is proposed.
