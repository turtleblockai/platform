## Fresh primary-source hunt

### OpenAI — provenance can establish origin without settling meaning

**Current relevant source checked:** OpenAI, *Advancing content provenance for a safer, more transparent AI ecosystem* (May 19, 2026; updated July 31, 2026)  
https://openai.com/index/advancing-content-provenance/

OpenAI describes a layered provenance approach using Content Credentials/C2PA, SynthID, and verification tooling to make the origin of supported AI-generated media more detectable.

**Matters for TurtleBlock:** provenance is necessary but not sufficient for today's problem. A system can correctly identify where a contribution came from while still semantically flattening what made the contribution different. TurtleBlock therefore needs both source provenance and a lossless translation boundary.

**Does not justify:** treating content-origin verification as proof that an interpretation is faithful; copying media-watermark architecture into learner dialogue; making Turtle the final authority on semantic equivalence.

**Fresher OpenAI signal also checked:** September 16, 2026 — *How workers are unlocking new ways of working*  
https://openai.com/index/unlocking-new-ways-of-working/

That research emphasizes that how tasks are divided and carried out alongside AI matters, and that people can incorporate activities outside their prior role into recurring workflows. It supports human agency and changing trajectories, but it is less directly useful than the provenance signal for today's narrow contract.

### NVIDIA — evidence, derived knowledge, correction, and action should not collapse

**Date:** September 4, 2026  
**Source:** NVIDIA, *Building a Memory-Driven Agent with NVIDIA NemoClaw*  
https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/building-a-memory-driven-agent-with-nvidia-nemoclaw/

NVIDIA's architecture separates source evidence from derived knowledge and governed action. A structured self-model contains derived judgments while a ledger records obligations, corrections, and audit events; user corrections remain inspectable rather than silently rewriting the past.

**Matters:** this strongly echoes the inherited edge. Human source material and Turtle's derived interpretation can coexist without being treated as the same object. A correction should become an attributable event, not silent normalization. Context can inform action without authorizing it.

**Does not justify:** generalized persistent personal memory; importing enterprise preference modeling into TurtleBlock learners; treating a self-model as the user's meaning; letting accumulated context authorize a merge.

### Minecraft Education — collaboration may have an authored convergence goal, but not by default

**Date:** September 14, 2026  
**Source:** Minecraft Education, *Every Voice Matters in Village Square*  
https://education.minecraft.net/en-us/blog/village-square-minecraft-civics

Village Square asks learners to encounter different perspectives, balance fairness and representation, explain reasoning, participate in shared decisions, and shape a village charter. Its family extension explicitly discusses compromise.

**Matters:** human difference can be made materially consequential inside a shared world. It also sharpens a boundary already exposed by plural foregrounds: compromise may be the explicit pedagogical purpose of one designed activity without becoming a hidden default objective for every TurtleBlock collaboration.

**Does not justify:** requiring a TurtleAsk perturbation to reconcile with an existing branch; treating disagreement as a temporary defect; letting the platform infer that consensus is the desirable educational endpoint.

### MIT Media Lab — values are situated and require reflection, not just compliance rules

**Date:** September 17, 2026  
**Source:** MIT Media Lab AHA Seminar, Daniella DiPaola, *A Virtue Ethics Approach to AI Literacy*  
https://www.media.mit.edu/events/aha-seminar-daniella-dipaola/

The seminar frames AI literacy around human choices about whether and how to use AI, the values guiding those choices, understanding the capabilities of the technology and the setting in which it is used, and reflection/adjustment after decisions rather than reliance on a checklist alone.

**Matters:** situated values cannot be safely collapsed into an abstract system rule. A human perturbation may carry value, context, or judgment that Turtle should preserve as source-distinct even while offering a computational interpretation.

**Does not justify:** inferring a participant's values from behavior; producing a machine-authored virtue profile; turning a human's situated response into an immutable preference record.

## Sanders ontology comparison

The immutable dissertation Dedoose source layer remains unchanged. Today's contribution is a later operational TurtleBlock artifact.

Relevant original Dedoose codes:

- **Freedom and Individuality** — a human contribution must not be normalized into the machine's prior frame merely because translation is technically easier.
- **Problem Posing Education** — a perturbation may create a new problem or contradiction rather than solve the Turtle's existing question.
- **Predetermined Outcomes** — canonical merge, consensus, coherence, or branch optimization cannot become hidden target states.
- **Shared Democracy** — human judgment retains authority over whether interpretations coexist, conflict, merge, or remain unresolved.
- **Social Impact** — translation determines whose language, values, and situated meaning survive computational representation.
- **Pedagogy** — preserving ambiguity, correction, refusal, and unresolved meaning is itself a pedagogical stance.
- **Observations on Life Itself** — situated human material can enter inquiry without first fitting a machine taxonomy.

Later Sanders/TurtleBlock concepts:

- **Co-active Emergence** — the human perturbation can genuinely change the trajectory while remaining human-authored.
- **Possible Possibles** — a perturbation can open a parallel or contradictory direction rather than an improved single answer.
- **Invisible Ideas Become Visible and Manipulable** — the source contribution and Turtle interpretation become separately inspectable objects.
- **Machine Responses as Material for Evaluation** — Turtle's translation becomes something a human may accept, contest, or reject.
- **Make, Walk Around In, and Change the Story** — a human perturbation can become a branch whose consequences are later inhabited.
- **Purposeful Play** — sideways, strange, humorous, impossible, or unresolved contributions can matter without being optimized into task completion.
- **Cohabitation Without Provenance Collapse** — human response, Turtle interpretation, branch state, and later world evidence remain distinguishable.
- **Human Perturbation / TurtleAsk** — migration 0009 already defines the human contribution and TurtleAsk boundary event; today's contract operationalizes the next representational boundary without modifying that source concept.

## Seven established CTC operational domains

This remains system-research infrastructure, not evidence of learner learning.

1. **Personal Inquiry — primary.** A human perturbation can redirect or complicate inquiry without being translated into Turtle's existing purpose.
2. **Compelling Problem or Question — primary.** A contradiction or sideways response may generate a new problem rather than answer the old one.
3. **Technology as Tool to Think With — primary.** Human source, Turtle interpretation, and WorldSpec effect become distinct manipulable computational objects.
4. **Formative Demonstration of Learning — supports.** Later pre/post trajectories may help interpret change, but trajectory change does not itself prove learning.
5. **Reflection as Learning — primary.** A learner can inspect and reject Turtle's interpretation while the original response remains recoverable.
6. **Social and Cultural Critique — primary.** Translation is a power-bearing act that can preserve or erase situated meaning, value, positionality, and ambiguity.
7. **Sharing and Collaborating — primary.** A human contribution can enter shared inquiry without compulsory assimilation, agreement, or merge.

No new CTC domain is proposed.

## Hostile regression suite

Twelve cases were created.

Valid cases include:

1. a human contradiction opening a parallel branch;
2. a sideways response producing a contested/proposed delta without forced merge;
3. a human rejecting Turtle's reading while preserving the source response;
4. no response causing no Turtle interpretation and no WorldSpec movement;
5. a later human-approved merge that still leaves source and interpretation separately addressable.

Intentionally invalid cases reject:

1. Turtle claiming semantic equivalence with the human response;
2. synthetic content substituting for a human perturbation;
3. silence causing a fabricated branch;
4. rewriting the human source during normalization;
5. a machine-required canonical merge;
6. Turtle interpretation pointing to the wrong source response;
7. dropping unresolved/ambiguous human difference during translation.

Core invariant:

> **lossless before normalized — the human perturbation remains recoverable and distinct from Turtle interpretation and WorldSpec effect.**
