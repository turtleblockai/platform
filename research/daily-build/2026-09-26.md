## Deliberation

Common signal: agency can be tested; a local test is not live-runtime proof; participant governance is not machine consensus; traceability does not require copying full conversations.

Rejected today:
- live persistent resurfacing behavior — authority semantics should come first;
- numeric nudge-depth scoring — no evidence supports a machine-defined amount of acceptable intervention;
- inferred revocation from mood or repeated refusal — authority should come from explicit human direction;
- generalized learner preference memory — unnecessary for this bounded edge.

Selected: a small authority contract representing human quieting, reduced initiative, stopped resurfacing, retired purpose, revoked delegation, explanation requests, and explicit restoration.
